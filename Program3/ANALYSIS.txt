ANALYSIS

The recursive formula begins by instantiating minCost[i][j] to represent
the minimum cost to travel from post i to post j, rentalCosts[i][j] to
represent the direct rental cost from post i to post j, and mid to 
designate an intermediate post between post i and post j. Then, the
formula used is minCost[i][j] = min(minCost[i][mid] + rentalCosts[mid][j])
for all i ≤ mid < j. For each value of mid, the cost is the sum of:
minimum cost to travel from i to mid, and the direct rental cost from mid
to j. My program then selects the mid that minimizes this resulting cost.

Theoretically, the outer loop runs over every possibly length from 2 to
totalPosts, producing a runtime of O(n). The middle loop goes over all 
potential starting posts for each route length, also resulting in a time 
complexity of O(n). Finally, the inner loop runs for each pair of starting
and ending posts to iterate over all possible intermediate posts. Since
the time complexity of this loop is also O(n), the total runtime is O(n^3).
